Audio Guide Tour for Bagdad Group

This week I had to work on creating audio guide for a fictional exhibition, by forking the Australian Center for Moving Image’s source code. Where they had made an audio tour for Scorsese’s one of the best movies directors, and definitely for me, a person that loves cinema, Scorsese is one of my favorites directors (I watched 6 of his feature movies, planning to watch at least 6 more).

I needed an idea for my audio guide and mathaf has an upcoming exhibition for bagdad modern art "What Lingers in the Artist Mind: Experiments and Legacies of the Baghdad Modern Art Group" which is opening on 25th of October which i thought would be a great chance to create an audio tour for.

While scanning through the project, I got a general understanding of the way it works. It was a good idea to use html, CSS and JavaScript only. It served the purpose by working up until this day. I cloned the repository and had my own version. I decided to have 6 stops in my audio guide.

  • Stop 1: Introduction
  • Stops 2-5: Paintings
  • Stop 6: The legacy of the Bagdad Group

I had to get the content that i wanted to use in my text-to-speech, I used the mathaf website as my resource, the page talking about the Bagdad group wasn't enough so i went to the biography pages that is in mathaf website, which was rich in information about the artist of the Bagdad group.
